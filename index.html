Game Introduction

Welcome to Living Under the Line. The goal of this game is to try and survive twelve months while living at the national poverty level. The budget summary keeps track of monthly expenses, while the personal situation reflects the current state of life. Monthly choices are options you can try and take to improve your situation. The game will end if you reach month 12, amass over $3000 in debt, lose your home, or become too sick to work.
