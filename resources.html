Further Help and Financial Education

Surviving on limited income is challenging, yet this is reality for millions of Americans daily, who live at or under the national poverty level. If you are able to, consider supporting those that are in need. Or, here are some resources to learn about finanical literacy. Even if you do not find yourself actively micromanaging finances from paycheck to paycheck, learning finanical literacy skills can still benefit.
